VOLUME MIXER 1.3 — PERSONAL MAC BUILD

INSTALL
1. Drag Volume Mixer into the Applications folder beside it.
2. Open the installed Volume Mixer app. A fader icon appears in the menu bar.
3. Choose Enable app control (or Resume control), then adjust a playing app.
4. If macOS asks for System Audio Recording access, choose Allow.

The sliders affect only the named app. Mute keeps the saved volume for later.
Use Play through below each slider to choose its output device. System output
follows the Mac's current output. A named device is remembered across reconnects.
Pause control and Quit restore each app's original volume and output.

UPDATING
Quit the old Volume Mixer from its gear menu, then drag the new copy to the same
Applications folder and choose Replace. Open the new installed app. Your saved
volumes and output choices are preserved. The gear menu shows Version 1.3.
Idle apps retain volume control after their rows disappear. Slider dragging is
smoother, temporary discovery errors preserve healthy routes, and large audio
backlogs recover with a short crossfade. Error rows now have a visible Retry button.
This includes the routing, metadata and packaging fixes from earlier versions.
macOS may ask for audio access again.
Click a slider, then use arrow keys for 1% changes or Shift–arrow for 10% changes.
Escape or a click outside closes the panel.

AUDIO CHECK
With control paused, gear > Check audio engine runs an optional offline check.
It uses synthetic audio in memory without capturing or playing hardware audio.

UNINSTALL
Open the gear menu and choose Uninstall Volume Mixer. Confirm to restore audio,
remove saved settings and the login item, move the app to Trash, and quit.
Empty Trash when you want to permanently remove the app.
Your source project and this downloaded disk image are not deleted.
macOS may retain its own permission/history entry after removal.

This local build is for this Apple Silicon Mac with macOS 27. It is ad-hoc signed,
not publicly notarized. It has no audio driver, daemon, network service, analytics,
or recording feature. Audio samples remain in memory on your Mac.

If a slider reports Control unavailable, click that text for the specific reason
and Retry control. Failed routes stay bypassed until you retry or the route changes.
If an output is disconnected, reconnect it or choose System
output in Play through. Pause control if playback doesn't sound correct.
Protected audio or unsupported device formats may bypass control; the app's
original audio remains available.
